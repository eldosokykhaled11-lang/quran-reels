Quran Reels – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. About the service

Quran Reels is a personal desktop tool operated by its owner, Mohamed Awad. It creates short videos of Quran recitation and publishes them to social media accounts owned by the operator (YouTube, Facebook Page, TikTok).

2. Who may use it

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer public sign-up and does not act on behalf of any other user.

3. Content

Quran text is taken unchanged from a verified Uthmani source. Recitations are by well-known reciters. No music or inappropriate content is added.

4. Third-party platforms

Publishing is done through each platform's official API. Use of those platforms is also subject to their own terms, including the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

5. Disclaimer

The tool is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind.
