Quran Reels – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Data we access

With the account owner's explicit permission, Quran Reels accesses only:

  • TikTok: basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos (video.publish).
  • YouTube / Google Drive: permission to upload videos and files to the owner's own channel and Drive.
  • Facebook: permission to publish videos to the owner's own Page.

2. How data is used

The data is used only to publish videos created by the tool to the owner's own accounts. No content is read from other users.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer only. They are never sent to any server other than the official platform APIs, and are never shared or sold.

4. Third parties

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data of anyone. No analytics or advertising trackers are used.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings (e.g. TikTok: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
